Unit: Telling Time & Routines

A Grammar I: Telling Time

When we want to ask about the time or schedules, we use the question words "What time" or "When" along with auxiliary verbs do or does.

Asking Questions:

  • What time is it? -> It's 11:00 (eleven o'clock).
  • When do you get up? -> At 7:00 (seven o'clock).
  • When does she get up? -> At 8:00 (eight o'clock).

Key Rules & Shortcuts:

  • Use "It's" when answering "What time is it?".
  • Use "At" when answering "When do/does...". (e.g., At seven o'clock).
  • Remember: it's = it is.

B Grammar II: Adverbs of Frequency

Adverbs of frequency tell us how often something happens. In second grade, we focus on always (100%) and never (0%).

Structure & Examples:

Subject Adverb Action / Rest Time Expression
I always brush my teeth every day.
She never watches TV in the evening.

Solved Examples (Step-by-Step)

Review these 15 solved examples across Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels based on Our World 2 units.

Basic Level

Example 1: Telling Time (O'clock)

Question: What time is it when the clock shows 1:00?

Solution: It's one o'clock.

Example 2: Telling Time (Question)

Question: Complete the question: "______ is it?" (Answer: It's 7:00)

Solution: What time is it?

Example 3: Time Preposition

Question: Fill in the blank: "I get up ______ seven o'clock."

Solution: at (I get up at seven o'clock.)

Example 4: Adverb "Always"

Question: Order the sentence: [I / always / go to school / in the morning]

Solution: I always go to school in the morning.

Example 5: Adverb "Never"

Question: Order the sentence: [I / never / eat breakfast / in the evening]

Solution: I never eat breakfast in the evening.

Intermediate Level

Example 6: Question with "Does"

Question: Complete: "When ______ he play with his friends?"

Solution: does (Singular third person 'he' requires auxiliary 'does').

Example 7: Third Person Routine Answer

Question: Answer the question: "When does she go to bed?" (Time: 8:00)

Solution: At 8:00 (eight o'clock).

Example 8: Adverb placement with third person

Question: Complete: "She studies. She ______ watches TV." (Meaning 0%)

Solution: never (She never watches TV).

Example 9: Combining time and frequency

Question: Write a sentence using: [I / always / eat lunch / at 12:00]

Solution: I always eat lunch at 12:00.

Example 10: Choosing 'at' or 'it's'

Question: Choose the correct word: "______ five o'clock. I'm eating lunch."

Solution: It's (Used at the start of a sentence telling the time).

Advanced Level

Example 11: Complex Routine Question

Question: Formulate the question for: "She studies in the evening." using 'What'.

Solution: What does she do in the evening?

Example 12: Multiple Adverbs Comparison

Question: Contrast two habits: "I ______ play video games in the afternoon, but I ______ go to school at night."

Solution: always (or never), never (Logical consistency with real-world routines).

Example 13: Time & Partner Dialogue

Question: Convert partner Q&A: "When do you get up?" -> "At 6:00." into a third-person statement for your sister.

Solution: My sister gets up at 6:00.

Example 14: Double Rule Integration (Time + Adverb)

Question: Translate routine into a full sentence: [Subject: I, Frequency: always, Action: eat breakfast, Time: at 7:00]

Solution: I always eat breakfast at 7:00.

Example 15: Error Correction

Question: Find and fix the error: "When do your sister go to bed?"

Solution: Change "do" to "does" -> "When does your sister go to bed?"

Basic Level Practice

1. Fill in the blank for the time: "It's nine ______." (9:00)

Step-by-step solution:

When writing digital or clock times in words for full hours, we use "o'clock". Therefore, the answer is o'clock.

2. Complete the time question: "______ is it?" -> "It's 5:00."

Step-by-step solution:

To ask for the current time, the correct phrase is What time. -> What time is it?

3. Choose 'at' or 'it's': "I get up ______ seven o'clock."

Step-by-step solution:

When specifying the exact time an action occurs, we use the preposition at. -> at seven o'clock.

4. Choose between 'always' and 'never': "I ______ go to bed in the morning." (0% chance)

Step-by-step solution:

Since people sleep at night and not in the morning, the correct adverb for 0% frequency is never.

5. Fill in the missing word: "I ______ go to school in the morning." (100% chance)

Step-by-step solution:

For daily school attendance (100% frequency), we use always.

Intermediate Level Practice

6. Complete the question: "______ do you eat lunch?" -> "At 12:00."

Step-by-step solution:

When asking about schedule time with "you", use the question word When. -> When do you eat lunch?

7. Fill in the correct auxiliary: "When ______ he play with his friends?"

Step-by-step solution:

The subject is third-person singular ("he"), so we use the auxiliary does.

8. Complete with the right adverb: "She studies. She ______ watches TV." (0% frequency)

Step-by-step solution:

To indicate zero frequency or negation of habit, use never.

9. Choose 'at' or 'it's': "______ one o'clock. I'm eating lunch."

Step-by-step solution:

At the beginning of a sentence stating the time, we use the contraction It's.

10. Unscramble: [always / brush / I / my teeth / every day]

Step-by-step solution:

Subject (I) + Adverb (always) + Verb phrase (brush my teeth) + Time expression (every day) -> I always brush my teeth every day.

Advanced Level Practice

11. Formulate the question for: "She studies in the evening." using 'What'.

Step-by-step solution:

What + auxiliary (does) + subject (she) + verb base (do) + time phrase (in the evening)? -> What does she do in the evening?

12. Complete with correct pronouns and auxiliary: "When ______ your sister go to bed?"

Step-by-step solution:

"Your sister" is singular third-person (she), so the correct auxiliary is does.

13. Translate routine into a complete sentence: [Subject: I, Frequency: never, Action: play video games, Time: in the morning]

Step-by-step solution:

Combine subject, adverb, action, and time: I never play video games in the morning.

14. Error correction: Find the mistake in "What time does you get up?" and correct it.

Step-by-step solution:

The pronoun "you" pairs with auxiliary "do", not "does". Corrected sentence: What time do you get up?

15. Combine time and frequency: Answer "When do you eat breakfast?" given that you always eat it at 7:00.

Step-by-step solution:

Incorporate frequency and time: I always eat breakfast at 7:00.

Critical Reflection & Logical Thinking

Discuss these open-ended questions with your teacher or classmate to deepen your conceptual understanding.

Question 1: Why do we use different auxiliary verbs ("do" vs "does") when asking about time?

Think about pronoun agreement. Why do we say "When do you get up?" but "When does she get up?" How does English grammar change for third-person singular subjects?

Question 2: How do adverbs of frequency help us describe our daily lives?

Compare your school days with your weekend days. Which activities do you always do, and which do you never do? Why are these words important for scheduling?

Question 3: Why is precision in telling time crucial in daily school routines?

Imagine if everyone arrived at school at random hours instead of specific o'clock times. How do clocks and time expressions keep our classroom organized?
